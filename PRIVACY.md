# Privacy Policy — Lumen

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

This Privacy Policy explains how **Lumen Labs, Inc.** ("we", "us", or "our")
collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Lumen mobile
application and website (collectively, the "Service"). This policy is written
to be a self-contained, publishable privacy notice suitable for the
Google Play Store and Apple App Store data safety forms.

By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of information in
accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

### A. Information you provide
- **Account:** display name, username, email, phone (optional), date of birth (for age gating), password (hashed, never stored plaintext), profile photo, bio.
- **Content:** videos, captions, comments, likes, live chat messages you publish.
- **Contacts / social invites** (only if you grant permission).
- **Payment information** processed by a PCI-DSS compliant provider (Stripe / Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase). We never store full card numbers.

### B. Information collected automatically
- **Device & usage:** approximate location (IP-based, optional), device model, OS, language, network type, screen size, app version.
- **Log data:** IP, referrer URLs, pages visited, timestamps.
- **Cookies / localStorage:** minimal, for session and preferences only.

### C. Permissions requested at runtime
- **Camera / microphone** — only when you start recording or join a LIVE room.
- **Photo library** — only when you upload content.
- **Notifications** — opt-in push alerts.
- **Contacts** — only if you choose "Find friends".

You can revoke any permission from your OS Settings at any time.

## 2. How we use information

- Provide, operate, and personalize the Service (feed ranking, recommendations).
- Process payments for subscriptions, virtual gifts, and creator payouts.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, spam, abuse, and safety incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
- Send service announcements, marketing (opt-in only), and support responses.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share health data,
precise location, or private messages with advertisers.

## 3. Sharing

We share information only with:

- **Service providers** who operate under contract: cloud hosting (AWS / GCP),
  payments (Stripe, Google, Apple), analytics (Plausible / PostHog), CDN,
  abuse-prevention tools.
- **Other users** for public content (public videos, public comments, public
  profile).
- **Authorities** only when required by a valid legal request (warrant, court
  order) and only after verifying its validity to the extent permitted by law.
- **Acquirers** in the event of a merger or acquisition, with notice to you
  and opportunities to delete your data before transfer.

## 4. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPL, etc.) you may
have the right to:

- Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal
  retention periods — typically 30 days for normal content, longer for
  financial records).
- Export your data via **Settings → Privacy → Download your data**.
- Object to or restrict certain kinds of processing.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, email `privacy@lumen.app`. We respond within 30 days.

## 5. Children

Lumen is **not directed to children under 13**. We offer separate "kids & teens"
profiles with restricted features and stronger moderation. If you believe a child
under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact
`privacy@lumen.app` so we can delete it.

## 6. Data retention

- Public content: retained while your account is active, plus a 30-day
  soft-delete window.
- Private messages: retained while your account is active, then deleted within
  30 days of account deletion.
- Financial records: retained for 7 years as required by tax and accounting law.
- Log data: retained for 90 days; aggregated for up to 2 years.

## 7. International transfers

Your information may be processed outside the country you live in, including in
the United States and the European Economic Area. We rely on Standard Contractual
Clauses (SCCs) and equivalent transfer mechanisms to protect your data.

## 8. Security

We use TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access controls, and regular
penetration testing. No system is 100% secure; in the event of a breach affecting
your data we will notify you without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies for session management. All analytics and
advertising cookies are opt-in via our consent banner.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app and by email.
Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
